Share of all 48 sites at each uplink tier, before the programme started vs. today.
At programme start, 50% of sites ran 1G uplinks, with several already pushing past 85% peak utilization. Today, 1G remains the most common single tier but its share has dropped from 24 to 22 sites as upgrades push more compounds to 2G and above. The share of sites at 2G or higher has grown from 10 to 11 sites. The remaining 1G sites are the lowest-traffic compounds, where an upgrade isn't yet justified by demand.
